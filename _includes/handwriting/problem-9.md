Two blocks are attached to each other by a taut string that runs over a
frictionless pulley. Block $M$ lies on an incline with angle $45^\circ$, and the
small block $m$ hangs vertically. The coefficient of friction between block $M$
and the surface is $0.135$. The mass of the small block is
$m=5.15\,\mathrm{kg}$. When the blocks are released, the large block $M$ gets
an acceleration of $2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ down the incline.
:::
