Three problems on logarithms from the mathematics exam V24. Solve them on
paper. For each problem you can

1. write the final answer in the answer field and choose **Check**. Write
   `ln(b)`, `sqrt(b)`, `x^2` and `e` for Euler's number; `2x` means $2\cdot x$.
   The check says whether the answer is right and as simple as the problem asks;
2. take a photo of your working, upload it under **Upload images** and choose
   **Feedback**. Each new click gives a deeper hint: first an assessment, then
   an explanation, then the method and finally a worked solution.
   The **Feedback language** menu next to each upload field chooses the
   language the AI answers in for that problem only.

The feedback comes from an AI model you choose yourself under the gear ⚙
(direct API with your own key, or “copy prompt” to an AI chat). The model must
be able to read images. The image and the problem text are sent to the provider
you choose. The AI can be wrong: read the feedback critically.

::: {.callout-note collapse="true"}
## For teachers: test with real answers

**Load student 1–9** loads real, anonymised answers from nine students
(handwritten in Norwegian), with the photo and the final answer, so you do not
have to upload anything. All nine answered 1b, seven answered 3b and six
answered 3c; a greyed-out number means that student did not answer. The
buttons at the top load every problem at once.
This page has its own prompts, separate from the physics page; see
[Playing with the prompts](handwriting-ai-prompts.qmd#the-logarithms-page). The
prompt sandbox at the bottom of the page lets you try changes without
rebuilding the site.
